# Attribution and scope

Conditional numerical fits to the CC BY 4.0 source dataset by Runze Lin et al., Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.20034288. Source locators and original archive/workbook hashes are preserved in evidence/microled-dynamic-20261029/source-ledger.json. Paper DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-74151-4 is cited for context; no paper figure is copied here.

These are independent normalized whole-chain magnitude fits. Phase, capacitance, lifetime, package, temperature and large-signal behavior are not identified. Coefficients do not activate a physical transient model.
